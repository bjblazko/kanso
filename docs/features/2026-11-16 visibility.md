# Who sees what

Being admin no longer means reading every space: one sees what oneself or
one's groups are entered for. Whoever creates a space owns it. Admins manage
every space under "Alle Bereiche" (members, name, deletion) without reading
it, and take over a space nobody can read with "Mich als Besitzer eintragen".
The visibility line names only people: "Nur für dich sichtbar", "Sichtbar
für dich, Anna", "Niemand hat Zugriff".

"Was andere sehen" (user menu) lists everything someone besides oneself
sees: wiki spaces, pages shared one by one, to-do and shopping lists, group
boards and meal plans, each with who that is. Notes are always private and
never listed.
