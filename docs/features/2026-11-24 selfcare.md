# Selfcare

A part for stressful moments, with four sections:

- **Erste Hilfe:** one random small step, with "Andere Idee" for the next one.
- **Akku-Check:** five levels with steps that suit them.
- **Übungen:** guided exercises — breathing 4–6 with counting, the senses
  5-4-3-2-1, window moment, body check, being kind to yourself, check-in,
  one good thing. The last two can be saved as a private note.
- **Hilfe von außen:** TelefonSeelsorge and 112 on every page.

Nothing is stored unless the person saves a note. The texts are Kanso's own.
