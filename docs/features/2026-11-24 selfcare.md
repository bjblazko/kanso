# Selfcare

A part for looking after yourself. It asks one question, „Was brauchst du
gerade?“, and offers three answers:

- **Kurze Pause** (`/care/between`): finished something? Close it, do something
  small and lifting for yourself, then arrive at what comes next (with „Fokus-Runde
  starten“). The answers can be saved as a note. The reminder (after 30/60/90
  minutes with Kanso open, per device; not during Fokus, a meditation or sounds)
  suggests this.
- **Zeit für mich** (`/care/me`): six meditations (3 to 15 minutes, spoken by Lyra
  or Orion in the UI language), Klänge (noise, waves, a calm chord, with a sleep
  timer; a later sleep area may take them over) and the exercises (breathing 4–6,
  senses, window, body check, kind to myself, check-in, evening review, one good
  thing).
- **Es ist gerade zu viel** (`/care/sos`): help from outside first
  (TelefonSeelsorge, 112), then one calming step with „Andere Idee“ and the way
  to breathe.

Own ideas (calming, lifting or both) and the reminder are under „Selfcare
einstellen“. Nothing about how someone feels is stored, except a note they
save themselves.
