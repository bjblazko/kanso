# Selfcare

A part for looking after yourself. It opens with four ways in: **Zwischendurch**
(a short step between two things: close, something lifting for you, arrive at
what is next), **Kurz durchatmen** (first aid), **Wie geht's mir?** (the
battery check, with a matching exercise) and **Zeit für mich** (meditations
and exercises). An explicit "Mir geht es gerade gar nicht gut" puts help
first. A reminder suggests Zwischendurch after 30/60/90 minutes while Kanso is
open, per device, and waits during Fokus or a meditation. Each person can add
their own ideas (calming, lifting or both). The sections:

- **Erste Hilfe:** one random small step, with "Andere Idee" for the next one.
- **Akku-Check:** five levels with steps that suit them.
- **Übungen:** guided exercises — breathing 4–6 with counting, the senses
  5-4-3-2-1, window moment, body check, being kind to yourself, check-in,
  one good thing. The last two can be saved as a private note.
- **Meditationen:** „Ankommen“ (5 min) and „Körperreise“ (10 min), spoken by
  Lyra or Orion (chosen on each start) in the UI language, with a gong at the
  start and the end and the text to read along.
- **Hilfe von außen:** TelefonSeelsorge and 112 on every page.

Nothing is stored unless the person saves a note. The texts are Kanso's own.
