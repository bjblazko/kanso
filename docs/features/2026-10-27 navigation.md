# Navigation: parts and content

A rail names the parts of Kanso (Notizen, Wiki; later Aufgaben, Listen,
Kalender) — at the bottom on phones, at the left on wide screens. The side bar
shows what is inside the current part (the wiki's spaces and page tree).
Group management lives in the user menu.
