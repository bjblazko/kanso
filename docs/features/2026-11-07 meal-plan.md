# Meal plan

A group's cooking plan under "Küche": a week from Monday to Sunday, meals
(recipes with servings) per day, added per day or with "Einplanen" on a
recipe. Chosen days go on a shopping list with the same preview as for one
recipe, amounts added up across meals; taking a meal off the plan offers to
take its ingredients off the list again. Third of three parts: recipes,
shopping list, meal plan.
