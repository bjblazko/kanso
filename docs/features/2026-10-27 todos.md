# To-do lists

Plain, flat to-do lists for small plans, mobile first and usable offline.
Items have one level of sub-items; ticked items stay in place and fade. The
overview lists your own and shared lists, most recently changed first (a tick
counts). The owner shares a list with people or groups, renames and deletes
it; everyone it is shared with ticks and edits items. Lists are not kept in
the Git history.

Tap an item's text to edit it; below it appear Indent/Outdent, Add sub-item
and Delete item.
