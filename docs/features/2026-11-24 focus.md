# Fokus (Pomodoro)

A Pomodoro timer as its own part: 25 minutes of focus, a 5-minute break, and
a 15-minute break after four rounds. The durations can be changed on each
device. Start/Pause, Überspringen and Zurücksetzen control it, and the next
phase waits for Start. At the end of a phase the open tab plays a soft gong and, if allowed, shows a
notification. Each break suggests a lifting Selfcare step. The
"Gedanken-Parkplatz" collects what comes up during a round and becomes a
private note on request. Nothing else goes to the server.
