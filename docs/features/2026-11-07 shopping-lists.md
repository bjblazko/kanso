# Shopping lists

A list under "Todos" can be a shopping list: amounts are typed with the item
("2 l Milch") and changed with − and +, each item can carry a note with
alternatives, and items filled from a recipe say what they are for. "Einkaufen"
on a recipe shows a preview with every ingredient ticked for the chosen
servings and adds them to a new or existing shopping list, adding up amounts of
the same ingredient. Second of three parts: recipes, shopping list, cooking
plan.
