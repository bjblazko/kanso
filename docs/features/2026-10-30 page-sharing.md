# Page sharing

A wiki page can be shared on its own with people or groups, with a role
(Lesen, Schreiben, Verwalten), as an exception to its space: "Timos
Wunschliste" for Christina without the rest of "Timos Space". Subpages
inherit the share; the sharing page says how many. Whoever gets a page this
way finds it under "Für dich freigegeben" in the wiki navigation and on the
wiki start page, and sees nothing else of the space: no other pages, search
hits, attachments or history. "Freigabe ändern" on a page leads to the page's
sharing.
