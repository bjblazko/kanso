# Berichte

A page in the user menu that shows what Kanso holds, who holds what and which
resources it takes: storage, what Git keeps of old versions and deleted files,
orphaned media, activity, the wiki's age and shape, tasks, kitchen and
sharing. Charts are drawn with d3; every chart has its numbers as a table.

Scopes: own documents, shared with me, all I see; admins also everyone, by
person — numbers only, titles only where they may read.
