# Move pages to another space

"Verschieben nach" on a page offers the other spaces too, each at the top
and below its pages, as far as the person may write there. The page moves
with its subpages, attachments and history (Git follows the folder); links
stay valid because they use the page id, and the page's own shares move
along. When the move changes who can see the page, a confirmation shows who
sees it until now and afterwards. A page that someone is editing (or a
subpage of it) does not leave its space until the editing is done.
