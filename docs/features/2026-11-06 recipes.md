# Recipes

A wiki page becomes a recipe with `type: recipe`: servings, tags, source and
time in the frontmatter, ingredients and steps in the text by a simple
convention. Kanso recognises amounts, units and names, shows how it read
them in the editor preview, offers a cook view for the phone at the stove
(servings scaled, ingredients and steps to tick off, screen kept on), and
lists all recipes the person may read under "Küche" with search (also by
ingredient) and tag filters. First of three parts: recipes, shopping list,
cooking plan.

Since 0.3.0 a recipe has a rating of one to five stars (`rating` in the
frontmatter, one value for the household, not per person). Whoever may
edit the recipe sets it on the recipe page; pressing the given stars again
clears it. The kitchen and the search filter by "ab 3 Sternen", "ab 4
Sternen" and "5 Sterne" and show the stars in the rows.

Recipes appear only in the kitchen: the wiki's spaces bar and subpage lists
leave them out, and an opened recipe belongs to Küche (2026-11-17). One space
is the cookbook: new recipes go there without asking, it is not in the wiki,
and its members decide who sees all recipes (ADR-016).
