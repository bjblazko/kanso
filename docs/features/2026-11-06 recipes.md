# Recipes

A wiki page becomes a recipe with `type: recipe`: servings, tags, source and
time in the frontmatter, ingredients and steps in the text by a simple
convention. Kanso recognises amounts, units and names, shows how it read
them in the editor preview, offers a cook view for the phone at the stove
(servings scaled, ingredients and steps to tick off, screen kept on), and
lists all recipes the person may read under "Küche" with search (also by
ingredient) and tag filters. First of three parts: recipes, shopping list,
cooking plan.
